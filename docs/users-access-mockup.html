Users & access

How this works:
  1. Seven roles, several per person. DEPT_HEAD is retired — its three holders keep product management as a menu grant, migrated automatically.
  2. "Can see spending for" and "Budget ownership" are separate sections. The first is visibility in the spend report, for anyone. The second is approval scope, for BOs only, at department + category grain. Seeing a department never lets you approve in it.
  3. Workflow menus follow the role and are locked. BO Approvals, CEO Approvals, Accounting, Procurement and Petty Cash do things, so they're granted by holding the role — never ticked separately. Ticking one alone would open a page where the person can't act.
  4. Viewing and settings menus are a free checklist, defaulting from the roles. An override — a grant the roles don't give, or a removal of one they do — is marked in terracotta, with a reset, so exceptions are visible rather than hidden.
  5. Submit and My Requests are always on for everyone, so they aren't listed.
  6. This replaces three things: the scattered multi-row roles table, the separate People & departments tab, and the settings_tab_permissions table.
