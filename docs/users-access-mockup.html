Users & access

How this works:
  1. Seven roles, several per person. DEPT_HEAD and PRODUCT_MANAGER are retired — their holders keep Products and Suppliers as menu grants, migrated automatically.
  2. Business unit belongs to the person — ONEST, SV or Both. Today it's read from any role row to stamp the BU on every request, so it can't live only on BO ownership. Both means the person picks the BU on each request; switching it clears categories already chosen.
  3. Anyone whose BU is blank today files as ONEST. The migration keeps that, but flags each one as BU defaulted so it gets confirmed rather than inherited.
  4. "Can see spending for" and "Budget ownership" are separate sections. The first is visibility in the spend report, for anyone. The second is approval scope, for BOs only, at department + category grain. Seeing a department never lets you approve in it.
  5. Workflow menus follow the role and are locked. BO Approvals, CEO Approvals, Accounting, Procurement and Petty Cash do things, so they're granted by holding the role — never ticked separately. Ticking one alone would open a page where the person can't act.
  6. Viewing and settings menus are a free checklist, defaulting from the roles. An override — a grant the roles don't give, or a removal of one they do — is marked in terracotta, with a reset, so exceptions are visible rather than hidden.
  7. Submit and My Requests are always on for everyone, so they aren't listed.
  8. Defaults match reality, so overrides stay rare. Suppliers includes Accounting, Categories is SUPERADMIN-only, and the spend report is open to every role — all as they work today. If every holder of a role needs the same override, the default is wrong. The only overrides left after migration are the three former DEPT_HEAD holders keeping Products and Suppliers.
  9. Settings opens only for people with at least one settings menu. Today a BO can reach Settings and find no tabs; that empty page goes away.
  10. Ten settings surfaces, not seven: Signature rules, Announcements and Petty cash custodians were missing from the first draft and are added with today's role defaults.
  11. This replaces three things: the scattered multi-row roles table, the separate People & departments tab, and the settings_tab_permissions table.
  12. Scope written on non-BO rows today — nine Petty Cash Custodians carry detailed department lists — is read by nothing. It's dropped rather than migrated, and recorded in docs/access-baseline.md so nothing is lost.
