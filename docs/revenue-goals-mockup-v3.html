Decisions in this mockup:
  1. Every level is addable — category, sub-category and channel, via the dashed buttons. Adding is data entry, not a code change, so a new store never needs a deployment.
  2. Months before a channel opens stay blank and read "not yet open", not 0. Siplor demonstrates it: goals from September only. A zero would drag averages down and imply a target that was missed.
  3. Closing a store deactivates it, never deletes it. Its historical goals must survive, or last year's comparison silently changes. A closed channel stops accepting new months and disappears from the BO's view once its goals are all in the past.
  4. Goals are typed only at channel level; sub-category, category, BU and company totals are all sums.
  5. With the BU filter on "Both", the denominator is both BUs combined.
  6. Cells are 116px, fitting 7 digits plus a percentage, fill-right on hover only.

  1. This structure is close to what a sales report would use. If actual revenue by channel ever lands in the portal, it should reuse these same channel rows rather than a parallel list — otherwise you get two channel hierarchies that disagree, which is exactly the problem the categories reconciliation just fixed.
